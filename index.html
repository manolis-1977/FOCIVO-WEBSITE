FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

What is FORYMO?

FORYMO is a personal productivity system built around Focus, Rhythm and Moments: Tasks support Focus, Routines build Rhythm, and Reminders with Today bring the right things into the right Moments.

Is FORYMO available in Greek?

Yes. FORYMO stays FORYMO in every language, while menus and explanations are localized into natural Greek based on meaning, not word-for-word machine translation.

Can I recover my data?

The Windows application includes a verified Backup & Recovery workflow designed to create, verify, preview and apply approved recovery backups.
