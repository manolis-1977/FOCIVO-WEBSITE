FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

What is FOCIVO?

FOCIVO is a personal productivity workspace that brings your daily view, tasks, routines, reminders and recovery tools together.

Is FOCIVO available in Greek?

Yes. FOCIVO stays FOCIVO in every language, while menus and explanations are localized into natural Greek based on meaning, not word-for-word machine translation.

Is the mobile app available now?

The mobile companion is in active development and will be released only after the required testing and acceptance steps are complete.

Can I recover my data?

The Windows application includes a verified Backup & Recovery workflow designed to create, verify, preview and apply approved recovery backups.
