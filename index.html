FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

What is FORYMO?

FORYMO is a personal productivity system built around Focus, Rhythm and Moments: Tasks support Focus, Routines build Rhythm, and Reminders with Today bring the right things into the right Moments.

Which languages does this website support?

The website is available in 12 languages. Choose your language from the menu to read the pages, guides and FAQ. Application language availability is separate.

Is the mobile app available now?

FORYMO Mobile is in active development and is not yet available for installation. It will be released only after the required testing and final acceptance.

Can I recover my data?

The Windows application includes a verified Backup & Recovery workflow designed to create, verify, preview and apply approved recovery backups.

Why is a task missing from Today?

Check the selected date in Today. Open Tasks and confirm the task’s scheduled date and status. Return to Today and refresh the view.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Check that the reminder is enabled and has the correct local date and time. Review sound and popup preferences in Settings. If the issue persists, check Diagnostics before contacting support.

How can I restore my workspace safely?

Open Backup & Recovery, verify the backup and review the recovery preview. Apply recovery only after confirming the proposed changes. Do not reset FORYMO as a first troubleshooting step.

What should I include when asking for help?

Include your preferred language, app version, operating system, the affected function and steps to reproduce the issue. Never send passwords, recovery keys, payment-card details or personal workspace content.
